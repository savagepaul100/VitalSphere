Clinical Status Guide

Meaning of FIT (Hb: 13.1 g/dL, Rest: 133d):

1. FIT (Medical Clearance)

The donor has successfully passed the mandatory pre-donation screening: pulse within 60-100 bpm, blood pressure (100-140 / 60-90 mmHg), non-reactive serology (HIV, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria), and absence of active medical deferrals.

2. Hb: 13.1 g/dL (Hemoglobin Threshold)

National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) regulations mandate a minimum of 12.5 g/dL. 13.1 g/dL confirms adequate oxygen-carrying red blood cell mass and prevents post-donation fatigue or anemia.

3. Rest: 133d (Cooldown Interval)

The statutory rest period between whole blood donations is 90 days (male donors) and 120 days (female donors) to restore body iron and ferritin reserves. 133 days verifies complete physiological recovery.

All donors undergo in-person verification by a licensed Medical Officer prior to donation.
