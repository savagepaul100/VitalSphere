Clinical Status Guide

What does FIT (Hb: 13.1 g/dL, Rest: 133d) mean?

• FIT: The donor has successfully passed pre-donation medical screening, vitals check, and serology verification.

• Hb: 13.1 g/dL (Hemoglobin): NBTC / NACO mandatory minimum standard is 12.5 g/dL. 13.1 g/dL confirms healthy oxygen-carrying capacity.

• Rest: 133d (Cooldown Interval): Mandatory recovery interval between whole blood donations is 90 days for male donors and 120 days for female donors. 133 days confirms full clinical recuperation.

All donors undergo mandatory physical examination by a registered Medical Officer prior to phlebotomy.
